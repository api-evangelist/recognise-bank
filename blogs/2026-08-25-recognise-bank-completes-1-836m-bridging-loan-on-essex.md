Recognise Bank has completed a £1.836m bridging loan to support the acquisition of a new commercial site in Harlow. The 18-month serviced bridging facility was completed at 57% LTV and was introduced by Commercial Manager, Matt Stevenson of Watts Commercial Finance. The mixed-use site includes residential accommodation, industrial units and yard space.
