Recognise Bank, the SME property lending Bank, has today increased its loan-to-value (LTV) on residential bridging finance from 75% to 80% with immediate effect. In addition, the Bank has introduced the use of short-form and automated valuations (AVMs) on selected residential bridging cases, helping to streamline customer journeys and improve speed of service.
