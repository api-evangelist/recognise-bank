This article was originally published in The Intermediary on 8 September 2026. Recognise Bank has completed a £9.9m bridging loan to refinance existing debt secured against two commercial properties in London and Glasgow. The 21-month facility, introduced by Raj Vilkhou of Express Financial Services, completed at 52% LTV.
