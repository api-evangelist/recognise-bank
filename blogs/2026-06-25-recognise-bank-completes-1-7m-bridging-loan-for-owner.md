Recognise Bank has completed a £1,697,500 bridging loan to support the acquisition of two industrial units in West Yorkshire. The borrower was acquiring premises from which its manufacturing business operates. The business has been established for more than 35 years and specialises in the development and manufacture of coatings, adhesives, elastomers and moulded rubber products.
