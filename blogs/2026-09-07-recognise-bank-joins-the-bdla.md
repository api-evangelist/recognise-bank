Recognise Bank has joined the Bridging & Development Lenders Association (BDLA) as a Lender Member. Recognise is a specialist bank providing property lending alongside business and personal savings products. Its bridging range supports residential and commercial property, including buy-to-let portfolios, HMOs, mixed-use and semi-commercial assets, commercial investments and owner-occupied business premises.
